# RecallTrace walkthrough video

**[RecallTrace-full-walkthrough.mp4](RecallTrace-full-walkthrough.mp4)** (17 minutes, 1440 × 900)
covers every section and operation of RecallTrace in 28 chapters. The file has chapter markers,
so players such as VLC or QuickTime can jump straight to a section.

Each step was checked automatically while it was recorded: **221 of 221 steps passed**. See
[walkthrough-test-report.md](walkthrough-test-report.md) for every chapter, its start time in the
video, and every step.

It was recorded on 15 November 2026 against a fresh local database with fictional companies
(Harbourline Foods (demo) and Lahore Knit Mills (demo)) and `.test` addresses. Notices were
written to a local mailbox; nothing was emailed.

One thing changed after recording. The customer notice in chapters 11–13 still shows the lot's
internal ID. Notices now name the lot by its code, SKU and quantity, for example
"lot LAMP-2611-B (C10-LAMP-01), potentially affected quantity 60 EA".

To record it again, see [`scripts/demo-video`](../../scripts/demo-video/README.md).
